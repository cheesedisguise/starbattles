how to play

  • Place stars so every row, column and outlined region holds the same number of stars (1, 2 or 3, set by the slider).
  • Stars can never touch, not even diagonally.
  • Tap a square to place a ★. Tap it again to remove it.
  • Tap a lock above a column or beside a row to lock it. The whole line turns red and can't be edited until you unlock it.
  • Press finish to check your answer. Ctrl Z undoes.
